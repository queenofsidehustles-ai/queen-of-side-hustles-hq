Quick Start Checklist

  • Set up your API keys in Settings to unlock AI features, content generation, and payments.

Sales Script Outline

  • Open with the problem: "Most small businesses are juggling 5-10 different tools for their website, CRM, email, content, and payments. That costs time and money."
  • Show the solution: Demo the dashboard -- contacts, deals, content creation, and store all in one place. Let them see it working live.
  • Highlight the AI: Show the content pipeline creating a real post in under 60 seconds. Show Jackie AI adding a contact with natural language.
  • Talk about ROI: "One new client per month from better content and follow-up pays for the entire system. Most businesses see that in week one."
  • Close with next steps: "Let's get your onboarding survey filled out today. We can have your system live within 48 hours."

Client Onboarding Checklist

  1. Business license / legal name -- needed for domain registration and payment processing setup.
  2. Domain name -- purchase or transfer their domain. Recommend Cloudflare Registrar for best pricing.
  3. Social media accounts -- get login access or admin roles for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn.
  4. Google Workspace -- set up professional email (name@business.com) and Google Business Profile.
  5. Stripe account -- create or connect their Stripe account for payment processing.
  6. 1Password vault -- shared vault for all credentials. Never store passwords in email or text.
